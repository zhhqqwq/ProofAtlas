## Gates

1. **Pre-answer state integrity** — no future/reference construction used as a search input.
2. **Required-Property stability** — the functional need survives harmless surface changes.
3. **Family-before-instance** — recover a candidate family, not a reference-specific formula.
4. **Parameter perturbation resistance** — changing convenient constants or error splits does not break the rationale.
5. **Isomorphic transfer** — recognize the mechanism under changed objects/notation/theorem surface.
6. **Constant calibration** — convenient specializations are not called necessary or optimal.
7. **Semantic route stability** — preserve the core route when structure is unchanged.
8. **Legitimate route change** — allow simplification when the variant genuinely removes a constraint.
9. **Reference blindness** — exact reference match is not evidence of M3.
10. **Human review** — no automatic M3 promotion.

## Hard failures

Any hard failure blocks promotion:

- hindsight leakage;
- mathematical invalidity;
- reference-specific constant mythology;
- candidate family defined by wrapping the answer;
- fabricated historical claim;
- failure on an isomorphic case whose mechanism is preserved.
