# Integrated Skill Corpus — Failure Pressure Report

These are not claims about controlled model failure rates. They are failure pressures repeatedly exposed during the 40-case batch generation and audit, and they directly determine Benchmark design.

## Highest-frequency pressures

- `over_archaeology_risk`: 5
- `theorem_black_box`: 5
- `magic_one_overinterpretation`: 3
- `over_m3_risk`: 3
- `policy_under_specification`: 3
- `canonical_memorization_risk`: 2
- `witness_origin_confusion`: 2

## F-01 Over-Archaeology

Low-construction proofs are easy to over-segment into Units/Episodes.

**Benchmark implication:** include explicit negative controls testing whether the Skill knows when not to expand.

## F-02 Theorem Black-Boxing

The failure is not merely forgetting a theorem name; it is failing to expose theorem preparation and local/global role.

## F-03 Magic-Constant Overclaim

Repeated around `1`, `1/2`, `1/n`, ε/2 and ε/3.

**Core error:** convenient specialization is narrated as necessary or optimal.

## F-04 Canonical-Proof Memorization

Fixed-point and differentiable⇒continuous cases show why masked recovery is not automatically M3.

Benchmark should include parameter perturbation or isomorphic transfer.

## F-05 Recursive Policy Under-Specification

A final subsequence is not enough. The explanation must preserve state, selection rule, invariant and progress measure.

## F-06 Witness-Origin Confusion

Existential witnesses such as ε0 must be separated from chosen scales such as 1/n.

## F-07 Quantifier / Causal Order Confusion

Examples include:

- N before δ;
- independent N1/N2;
- presentation order versus search order.

## F-08 Reference-Route Bias

Alternative-route cases show that a reference contradiction proof must not erase a valid EVT route, or vice versa.

## Consequence

Pattern Library should organize **transferable mechanisms**.

Benchmark should attack **failure pressures**.

They should not be duplicate chapter taxonomies.
